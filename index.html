Experiment 1: Problem Statement

Cinetix – Online Movie Ticket Booking

1. Background & Existing System

Traditional physical box-office setups require customers to wait in long queues, offer limited visibility into seat layouts, and rely heavily on paper tickets. Reconciliation of ticket revenues is manual and prone to human errors.

2. Proposed System Objectives

  • Automate movie browsing, seat selection, and checkout.
  • Provide an interactive real-time graphical seat layout.
  • Prevent double bookings using transient database locking.
  • Generate digital e-tickets with verifiable QR codes.
  • Provide cinema admins with robust box-office analytics.

3. Final Problem Statement

"The proposed Cinetix platform provides a centralized, automated web application for cinema ticketing and theatre operations. It enables moviegoers to view trailers, select showtimes, pick seats from an interactive real-time floor plan, and book digital tickets securely online. Simultaneously, it equips theatre administrators with tools to configure multi-screen auditoriums, manage schedules, adjust ticket pricing, and analyze box-office revenue in real time."

Mobile Stock System – Retail POS & Inventory

1. Background & Existing System

Retail mobile shops often manage inventory using paper registers or generic spreadsheets. Tracking 15-digit IMEI numbers manually leads to clerical errors, inventory leakage, warranty complications, and stockouts of fast-moving products.

2. Proposed System Objectives

  • Provide strict unit-level tracking of devices via unique 15-digit IMEI verification.
  • Automate stock inwarding and real-time deductions upon sale.
  • Expedite checkout using USB barcode/IMEI scanners.
  • Generate GST-compliant tax invoices displaying purchased IMEIs for warranty.
  • Maintain supplier purchase logs and generate gross margin reports.

3. Final Problem Statement

"The proposed Mobile Stock System is an integrated inventory and point-of-sale platform developed specifically for mobile electronics retailers. It automates inventory inwarding, tracks individual devices via barcode scanning, provides automated low-stock warnings, and enables rapid counter billing. It minimizes stock discrepancies, protects against inventory leakage, and provides store owners with complete visibility over profits and warranty claims."

SRS: Cinetix Platform

Functional Requirements

  • FR1: System shall authenticate users and admins (JWT).
  • FR2: Display movies with posters, synopses, and showtimes.
  • FR3: Present an interactive 2D visual seat layout.
  • FR4: Temporarily lock seats for 10 mins during checkout.
  • FR5: Generate e-ticket with a unique verifiable QR code.
  • FR6: Admins can manage screens, shows, and pricing tiers.

Non-Functional Requirements

  • Security: Passwords hashed (bcrypt); HTTPS enforced.
  • Performance: Seat map loads in < 1.5 seconds.
  • Concurrency: DB transactional locking to prevent double booking.
  • Availability: 99.5% uptime during peak ticket releases.

SRS: Mobile Stock System

Functional Requirements

  • FR1: Role-based access (Admin, Stock Manager, Cashier).
  • FR2: Enforce strictly unique 15-digit IMEI verification.
  • FR3: Real-time automated low-stock dashboard alerts.
  • FR4: Instant barcode scanning for POS invoice generation.
  • FR5: Automatic calculation of CGST, SGST, and net totals.
  • FR6: Generate daily gross margin and dead-stock reports.

Non-Functional Requirements

  • Data Integrity: Strict DB unique constraints on IMEI records.
  • Performance: Scanner lookup completes in < 500ms.
  • Security: Cashiers cannot view wholesale purchase costs.
  • Usability: Keyboard shortcuts support for rapid billing.
